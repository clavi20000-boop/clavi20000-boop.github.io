Physics of the Logos: Orthodox Responses to Liquid Modernity

by Jorge Clavijo Hernández

An interdisciplinary book examining Orthodox Christian responses to modernity, bringing theology, philosophy, metaphysics, and questions raised by the sciences into a single conversation.

Written for readers interested in Orthodox Christian thought, philosophy of religion, metaphysics, the relationship between science and theology, and contemporary cultural questions.
